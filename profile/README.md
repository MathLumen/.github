## Where mathematics meets discovery

We believe that mathematics is more than a collection of equations. It is a language for understanding the structures, patterns, and principles that shape our world.

</div>

MathLumen is an independent mathematics publication and research initiative exploring the connections between advanced mathematics, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and scientific computing.

Our goal is to bridge theoretical mathematical ideas with modern computational applications through research-driven writing, open-source tools, and educational resources.

We bring together mathematical thinking, scientific exploration, and technology to make complex ideas more understandable, useful, and accessible.

---

## Our research interests

### 01 · Mathematical Foundations

Real analysis, functional analysis, linear algebra, probability, optimization, and the mathematical structures underlying modern scientific methods.

### 02 · Applied and Computational Mathematics

Numerical analysis, differential equations, numerical optimization, mathematical modeling, computational methods, and scientific simulation.

### 03 · Scientific Machine Learning

Physics-informed neural networks, operator learning, scientific computing, reduced-order modeling, and data-driven approaches to scientific problems.

### 04 · Machine Learning and AI

Deep learning theory, neural networks, mathematical foundations of learning, geometric deep learning, and the mathematical principles behind modern AI systems.

### 05 · Research and Discovery

Emerging mathematical developments, research insights, interdisciplinary connections, and ideas at the frontier of mathematical science.

### 06 · History and Philosophy of Mathematics

The people, questions, discoveries, and intellectual journeys that have shaped mathematical thought.

## A mathematical perspective

Consider the heat equation:

```math
\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = \nu \Delta u
```

This fundamental equation describes diffusion across a wide range of scientific systems.

Through Fourier analysis, numerical discretization, and computational modeling, we can transform this mathematical structure into practical methods for understanding and simulating complex phenomena.

The same mathematical foundations connect differential equations, numerical analysis, scientific machine learning, and modern computational science.

These connections are central to the questions we explore at MathLumen.

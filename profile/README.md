# MathLumen

### Illuminating Mathematics  
**Mathematics × Machine Learning × Artificial Intelligence**

MathLumen is an open research initiative dedicated to exploring and advancing the mathematical foundations behind modern machine learning and artificial intelligence.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between deep theoretical mathematics and real-world AI systems — making complex ideas accessible, insightful, and impactful.

We build open-source tools, publish research-driven content, and create educational resources for students, researchers, and engineers.
